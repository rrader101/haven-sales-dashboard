Market

Booked → invoiced → collected

Where the money for this scope stands. Booked is approved deal Amount, Invoiced is Invoiced Amount, Collected is Paid Amount — all from the deals attached to the issue. The shortfall at each step is what has not moved yet.

Markets in this slate

Every issue printed on this slate, sorted by collection rate. The bottom of this list is where a slate's billing is leaking.

This market over recent slates

The same market across the last slates, so you can see whether this issue is normal for it. The selected issue is highlighted.

Invoice status

Every invoice raised against this scope's deals. In this org the invoice record is the Quote object — one per deal, its Invoice # matching the deal's. Voided invoices were cancelled and usually re-cut.

Open invoice aging

Invoices still awaiting payment, bucketed by how far past their due date they are. Needs a live Salesforce connection — due dates are not part of the bundled snapshot.

Every deal on this issue

The billing line for each approved deal: what it was booked at, what was invoiced, what has been paid, the invoice number and its status. Search the table to find a single advertiser.

Revenue by product

Line-item revenue on this scope's approved deals. Products priced at $0 — identifiers like Camera-Ready and Re-Run — carry volume but no revenue.

Extra copies

Line items whose product name contains Extra Copies, for this scope — copies moved and dollars billed for them.

Deals marked "Stop Invoice"

The Prepaid__c field (labelled Stop Invoice) set to Yes on an approved deal — billing is deliberately suppressed, usually because a subscription already covers it or it was paid up front.

This tab ignores the picker — it is the whole book over time, with the selected slate marked so you can place it in context.

Booked vs invoiced vs collected, per slate

Every slate over the last two years. The green series only begins where Paid Amount started being written (the move onto the Xero/Stripe sync), and the last few slates have not finished collecting.

Realization rate over time

Invoiced rate = invoiced ÷ booked. Collection rate = collected ÷ booked. The invoiced line runs the full window; the collection line starts at the billing-system cutover.

Refunds over time

Refunded Stripe invoices by the date Stripe created the original invoice.

Refunds by originating slate

Each refunded invoice traced through its agreement to that agreement's first deal, and then to that deal's issue launch date — where the relationship started, not necessarily the issue refunded.

Subscriptions and collections sit at agreement level, spanning many issues, so they are not filtered by the picker.

Stripe subscriptions by status

Live subscription records. Monthly price sums the recurring price on each, so the Active row is the closest thing to current MRR in the org.

Collections queue

Agreements by Collections Status. Value is the agreement's total value; collected is total paid against it.
